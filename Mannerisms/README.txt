Mannerisms - radial roleplay menus for World of Warcraft: Forever

Hold a key and a ring of emotes opens around your cursor. Move toward a
slice and let go: your character does it. Inspired by OPie, made for RP.

USING IT
  1. Type /mm and click "Key 1" on the Mannerisms ring, then press the key
     you want (for example Shift-E). Or use Options > Keybindings >
     AddOns > Mannerisms.
  2. Hold that key, flick the mouse toward a slice, let go.
  - A quick tap leaves the ring open; then click a slice.
  - Letting go in the middle cancels. Right-click goes back a ring,
    Escape closes it.
  - Slices with a small arrow open another ring (Greetings, Tavern...).

WHAT /me IS
  /me (or /e) describes what your character does, in third person, with
  your name in front. "/me leans on the bar." shows "Raptor leans on the
  bar." in orange to everyone nearby. Roleplayers use it for actions,
  expressions and anything that isn't speech; /say is for what you say.

SLICES
  Emote (/me)  - one of its lines is picked at random each time
  Say          - spoken in /say
  Game emote   - the game's own /wave, /bow, /cheer... with animation
  Open ring    - a sub-menu

  Lines can use {target} {first} {last} {full} {title} {race} {class}
  {he} {him} {his} {himself}. Your name and pronouns come from your
  Total RP 3 profile when you have one. {target} is your target's name,
  or "the dwarf man" if Well Met says you haven't met them yet. A line
  that needs a target is skipped when you have none.

EDITING
  /mm opens the editor: rings on the left, slices in the middle, the
  slice on the right. Up to six rings can have their own key; any ring
  can also sit inside another. The ring marked * is the main one.
  /mm open      open the main ring for clicking
  /mm reset     put the default rings back
